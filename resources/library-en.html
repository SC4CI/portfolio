Why did I build this library?

I built it as a fast personal reference instead of repeatedly searching through many separate files and notes. I collected the penetration-testing topics I study and organized them by use so I can quickly reach core commands and steps during learning and review. The content continues to evolve as I progress, so I treat it as a personal learning and review library—not a replacement for original references.
